How costs build up

Claude re-reads the whole conversation on every reply, and keeps a low-cost copy of it for only a few minutes.

1

Cost creeps up. Every reply re-reads everything said so far, so each message costs a bit more than the last.

2

A break makes it jump. After a few minutes away, the low-cost copy expires and the next message reloads it all at full price.

3

Starting fresh resets it. A new conversation with a short summary costs a fraction of continuing.

Long conversation

You've covered a lot of ground in one chat.

What to do

Continue only if it's the same topic. New topic? Start a new conversation.

Why it helps

Old, unrelated history is paid for again on every message.

Large conversation

The conversation has grown big.

What to do

Same task: ask Claude to shorten the history (/compact). New task: start fresh.

Why it helps

Every reply costs more just to re-read it all.

Almost full

Claude is near the most it can hold at once.

What to do

Ask for a short handoff note, start a new conversation, paste it in.

Why it helps

Claude gets less precise when full, and replies cost the most.

Over your limit

This conversation passed the spending limit you set.

What to do

Finish the step, ask for a 3-line summary, start fresh with it.

Why it helps

Stops one conversation from quietly running up the bill.

Breaks and timing

Five-minute timer info

A countdown until the low-cost copy expires.

What to do

Put follow-up questions in one message and reply before it runs out.

Why it helps

Replying in time keeps every message at the low price.

Timer ran out

The panel turns red after a break.

What to do

Start a new conversation with a 2–3 line summary instead of continuing.

Why it helps

Continuing reloads the whole conversation at full price.

Timer ran out several times

A running count of breaks and what they cost.

What to do

Take breaks at natural stopping points, then start fresh.

Why it helps

Each expiry pays for the whole conversation again.

Woken up while you were away

Background helpers finished after the timer ran out.

What to do

Wait for helpers to finish before stepping away, or stop them first (Esc).

Why it helps

Each late wake-up reloads everything at full price.

Unexpected reset

The low-cost copy was thrown away mid-conversation.

What to do

Pick your model and settings at the start; don't switch midway.

Why it helps

Switching discards the copy and pays to rebuild it.
